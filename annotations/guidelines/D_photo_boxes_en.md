# Annotation D: are the photo boxes right? (guideline v1.2, 2026-10-18) — English translation

English translation (2026-10-28) of `D_photo_boxes_zh.md`, the guideline the student annotator used. The Chinese
version is authoritative. "Dr Huang" is the author (Eden).

## What you see
A whole page of a company's sustainability report. The program has drawn a box around every image it found.
Some boxes are around charts, logos or icons; that is fine, ignore them.

## What to do
Look at every real **photo** on the page (the same meaning as in annotation A: a real photograph taken with a
camera).
- It has exactly one box, and the box fits: do nothing.
- It has **no box**: draw a box yourself and label it **Missed photo**.
- The box covers only part of it: click that box and choose **Partial**.
- One box covers two or more photos: click that box and choose **Merged**.
- One photo is cut into pieces by several boxes: click one of them and choose **Split**.

If the page has no photo at all, tick **No photos on this page**.

## Before you start
1. Read the example page.
2. Do 10 practice pages. After each page you see the correct answer. You need 9 correct to start the real pages;
   otherwise read the examples again and do another set of practice pages.

## Quality checks
- Pages come in batches of 50. We check each batch before opening the next.
- Some pages have known answers and are used to check quality. If too many are wrong, the batch is redone.

## Rules
- About 15 to 20 seconds per page.
- Do not discuss items with other annotators.
- Use a browser on a laptop or desktop, not a phone.
- Ask Dr Huang if anything is unclear.

# Annotation C: find the same photo in the previous year's report (guideline v1.4, 2026-10-21) — English translation

English translation (2026-10-28) of `C_find_in_last_year_zh.md`, the guideline the student annotator used. The
Chinese version is authoritative. "Dr Huang" is the author (Eden).

## What you see
- Top left: a photo from a company's sustainability report. Top right (blue box): the photo in the same
  company's report of the previous year that **the program guesses is the same one**.
- Below: the other photos from the previous year's report that resemble it, about 10 to 25, in page order.
  Click 🔍 to enlarge.

## What to do
1. Look at the two photos at the top first: is the one on the right the same photo (or the same scene shot from
   about the same position)? Press **1 yes** or **2 no**. The program is often wrong; do not choose "yes" just
   because the program guessed it.
2. Then look below: click any photo that is the same photo or the same scene (several can be clicked). If none,
   click nothing.
3. Press Enter for the next item.

## Before you start
1. Read the example page.
2. Do 5 practice items. After each answer you see the correct answer. You must find all 5 to start the real items.

## Same photo or same scene (the same rule as annotation B)
- **Counts**: the same photo, even if cropped, scaled, mirrored, colour-adjusted, compressed, partly covered by
  text or graphics, or placed inside a collage. Also another shot of the same scene: the same place, about the
  same angle, the same main content.
- **Does not count**: a different scene; the same kind of activity but different people or a different place;
  the same place but a clearly different angle; the same backdrop but different people or a different event.

## Tips
- Look through the whole grid before deciding. The matching photo may be small, mirrored, recoloured, or only
  one tile of a collage.
- The thumbnails are in the report's page order; the order is not a hint.
- The program only picked photos that "look similar"; many items have no match at all: answer "no" at the top
  and click nothing below, and that means "no match".
- About 15 seconds per item.

## Versions

## Quality checks
- Items come in batches of 20. We check each batch before opening the next.
- Some items hide a known matching photo among the thumbnails to check quality. If too many are missed, the batch
  is redone.

## Rules
- Use your eyes only; do not search the web or use other tools.
- Do not discuss items with other annotators.
- Use a browser on a laptop or desktop, not a phone.
- Ask Dr Huang if anything is unclear.
